How to read these scores

Propensity estimates the target Y: how likely this customer is to fit a candidate product they do not already own. X variables include segment and archetype, customer profile fields, balance and transaction behavior, channel usage, financial need signals, current product portfolio depth, and product/category descriptors.

Segment prior is the baseline product tendency for the customer's behavioral segment. Max affinity is the strongest historical link between any product the customer already owns and the candidate product. Recommendation score is the stored ranking score used to order eligible products; it is not a calibrated probability.

X variables used by the propensity model
Customer profile age, tenure_days, income_decile, customer_class, csp, employment, employment_detail, income_schedule, location
Balance and transactions starting_balance, total_deposit, total_withdrawal, net_cash_flow, avg_balance, max_balance, min_balance, balance_volatility, balance_growth, txn_count, withdrawal_deposit_ratio
Behavior and needs salary_day, payday_week_of_month, is_tre, channel_entropy, digital_tx_pct, atm_tx_pct, branch_tx_pct, financial_capacity, digital_affinity, credit_need, savings_affinity, international_transfer_need, business_complexity, relationship_depth
Segment, product, and portfolio segment_product_prior, product_prior_multiplier, product_model_segment, segment, latent_archetype, category, product, family, max_owned_to_candidate_affinity, mean_owned_to_candidate_affinity, n_linked_owned_products, n_other_owned_products, n_other_owned_families
